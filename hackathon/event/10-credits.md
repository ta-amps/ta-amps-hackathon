# Credits

## $150 Meta Model API credit

Every accepted participant can claim $150 in Meta Model API credits. The Oct 26 email (7:26 AM ET) explains the steps.

| Step | What to do |
|---|---|
| 1 | Apply through the claim form linked in the Oct 26 credits email. The Credit Terms say the form asks for your name, application email and your account's **Team ID** |
| 2 | Credits are applied in batches. Check the **Billing** page of your Model API account |
| 3 | Put a **card on file**. You need one to use credits and make API calls |
| 4 | Set a **Spend Limit** notification on the Billing page if you don't want to spend past $150 |
| Deadline | Claim by **Wed Nov 4, 9:00 PM ET** (Nov 5, 02:00 UTC) |
| Who claims | Each person claims their own: solo entrants, every team member and startup entrants. Nobody can claim for someone else |

## Things that are easy to miss

- **Validity conflict.** The Official Rules describe the credit as valid for "a 10-day development window"; the Credit
  Terms say credits "do not expire". Plan as if you have ten days.
- **Overage is billed.** Once the credit is used up, usage is charged at standard prices to the card on file.
- **Geography.** You must live in a country on Meta's Geographic Use Policy list. See
  [13-acceptable-use-and-data.md](13-acceptable-use-and-data.md).
- **Optional.** Credits don't affect judging, and you can build with Meta's open-weights models without a Model API account.
- **The Credit Terms PDF** is linked from the waiver page through a signed link that expires. Save your own copy for your records.
