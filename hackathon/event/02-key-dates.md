then Eastern time is really UTC−4 (EDT). The kickoff and the submission deadline below use each day's real offset.

## Things to know

- Dates "may be subject to change at Meta's sole discretion"; changes are posted on the event page.
- The submission platform marks anything after the deadline as late, and changing your repository after the deadline
  also marks the project late. See [11-submission.md](11-submission.md).
- Submit well before the deadline. A midday Nov 4 target leaves room for upload problems.
