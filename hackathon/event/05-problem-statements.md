open-weights models, and to code with the Muse Code agent. The Cerebral Valley page also names Muse Wonder. Model ids

## Choosing a theme

Kit guidance, not an official rule.

- Lead with the theme that matches your main outcome. **Build for Your World** fits a problem you know firsthand in
  your own field or community. **Push the Frontier** fits hard technical ground such as software, robotics,
  manufacturing, energy or materials. A project can lean on both; pick one to lead with.
- State the fit in one plain sentence near the top of your description: the theme, who benefits and what changes
  for them. For example, "Build for Your World: your-app helps a small bakery plan the week's orders in ten minutes
  instead of two hours", or "Push the Frontier: my-api lets a lab robot sort samples it has never seen before."
- Use the same sentence in your demo video and README, so judges hear one story.
- Re-check both event pages before you submit; the organizers can change them.
