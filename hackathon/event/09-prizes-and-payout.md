# Prizes and payout

## Prize pool: $1,000,000

| Place | Indie developer track | Startup track |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | $150,000 | $250,000 |
| 2nd | $100,000 | $150,000 |
| 3rd | $75,000 | $75,000 |
| 4th | $50,000 | $50,000 |
| 5th | $25,000 | $25,000 |
| **Total** | **$400,000** | **$550,000** |

**Bonus awards:** up to $50,000 in total for standout projects or community achievements. Categories may include a
community vote award and a most viral project award, among others the organizers name. They're announced on or about
Nov 10 and don't reduce the track prizes. (The submission platform showed public voting switched off on Oct 26.)

## How winners get paid, in our words

| Topic | What the rules and payout notes say |
|---|---|
| Currency | US dollars, the currency the pool is funded in |
| Provider | Paid through a payout provider; Cerebral Valley currently uses Tremendous, with bank payout options. Winners enter bank details with the provider directly |
| Verification | You're not a winner until your eligibility is verified and you're told verification is complete |
| Timing | Within 30 days of verification and of receiving any documents they need, once Meta has funded the prize |
| Teams | One payment to the Representative or a split among members; the team decides and confirms in writing. The organizers aren't liable for team disputes |
| Taxes and fees | Winners pay wiring, currency-exchange and banking fees and all taxes. Expect a W-9 (US) or W-8BEN (non-US) form; US withholding and reporting may apply |
| Publicity | Winners' names, likenesses, bios and statements may be used to describe the event, without further consent or pay |

Nothing here is tax advice; ask a tax professional about your situation.
