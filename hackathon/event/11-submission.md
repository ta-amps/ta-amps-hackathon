## Late rules

- The platform has a short grace setting, but anything submitted after the deadline is **marked late**, and hosts "may
  consider that during judging". Don't plan on the grace period.
- **"Changing the repository after the deadline will mark this project late."** Freeze your repo at the deadline: no
  pushes, no force-pushes and no new tags afterwards. If you want to keep working, do it on a branch or a fork you
  don't submit.

## Checklist

- [ ] Signed in and checked the form's fields at least two days ahead
- [ ] Repository link works for the judges (public, or shared as the form requires)
- [ ] Live project link works with the network slow or flaky; an offline or snapshot mode helps
- [ ] Demo video uploaded and playable in a private window; captions on
- [ ] Description covers what it does, who it helps, which Meta tools do what, what existed before the event, which other
      tools you used, and the limits
- [ ] Every team member listed
- [ ] Credits claimed by each person who needs them
- [ ] Submitted by midday Nov 4 ET, with the confirmation saved
- [ ] Repository frozen at the deadline
