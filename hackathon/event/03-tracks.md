# Tracks

Every project enters exactly one track, and each person enters one track only. Both tracks share the same two themes
and the same four judging criteria; the event page adds a different emphasis for each.

| | Indie developer track | Startup track |
|---|---|---|
| For | Individual builders and students | Technical founders building a product |
| Team | Solo or a team of up to 4 | A startup team |
| Who submits | The team's Representative, who lists every team member | The startup's Representative, who must be an active employee authorized to receive notices and prizes for the startup |
| Event-page emphasis | "Creativity, technical ambition, and whether it works" | "Technical execution and whether it holds up in production" |
| Prize pool | $400,000 | $550,000 |

## Prizes by place

| Place | Indie | Startup |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | $150,000 | $250,000 |
| 2nd | $100,000 | $150,000 |
| 3rd | $75,000 | $75,000 |

Bonus awards of up to $50,000 in total sit on top of both tracks. See [09-prizes-and-payout.md](09-prizes-and-payout.md).

## The Representative

A team appoints one Representative. That person must meet the individual eligibility rules, submits the project,
names all team members, and confirms that every member has read and agreed to the Official Rules. Prize splits
within a team are the team's job, not the organizers'.

## Switching tracks

The application form said "you can change your track anytime". The Official Rules still allow one track per person and project.
Ask on Discord if you need to switch late.
