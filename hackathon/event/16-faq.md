# FAQ (unofficial)

Our answers, each pointing at the section it comes from. When in doubt, ask on Discord and keep the answer with its date.

**When exactly is the deadline?**
Nov 4, 9:00 PM ET, which is 2026-11-05 02:00 UTC (clocks fall back on Nov 1). See [02-key-dates.md](02-key-dates.md).

**Can I keep pushing to my repo after submitting?**
Before the deadline, yes. After it, changing the repository marks the project late. See [11-submission.md](11-submission.md).

**Does my repo have to be public?**
The rules don't say so. The form asks for a GitHub repository, so make sure judges can open it. See [11-submission.md](11-submission.md).

**How long can the demo video be?**
No length is published (as of Oct 26). Short and complete wins attention; three minutes is a common target. See [11-submission.md](11-submission.md).

**Can I build on code I wrote before the event?**
The rules require original work that you own, and allow open-source components when you follow their licences and your
submission builds on them. There's no rule that all code must be new. Say clearly what existed before. See [07-official-rules-summary.md](07-official-rules-summary.md).

**Can I use Claude Code, Copilot or other AI coding tools?**
The rules don't mention them. They allow third-party technical help if the parts stay your own work. Disclose the tools
you used; Meta's tooling is still 25% of the score. See [07-official-rules-summary.md](07-official-rules-summary.md) and [08-judging.md](08-judging.md).

**Do I have to use Meta's models?**
No clause forbids other models, but "Use of Meta AI Tooling" is 25% of the score. See [12-models-and-tools.md](12-models-and-tools.md).

**How big can an Indie team be?**
Up to four people, with one Representative who submits. See [03-tracks.md](03-tracks.md).

**Who owns my project?**
You do. The organizers get a broad non-exclusive licence to use it, including in marketing. Anything else you share during
the event isn't confidential. See [07-official-rules-summary.md](07-official-rules-summary.md).

**Do the $150 credits expire?**
The rules say "a 10-day development window"; the Credit Terms say they don't expire. Plan for ten days. See [10-credits.md](10-credits.md).

**Is the kickoff recording available to download?**
Recordings belong to the organizers. Watch the official stream or replay; don't rip or republish it. See [../events/README.md](../events/README.md).

**Can I scrape Facebook or Instagram for my project?**
No, not without Meta's written permission. Meta Content Library is the research route, and it needs an affiliation
and a review. See [13-acceptable-use-and-data.md](13-acceptable-use-and-data.md).

**Can my Model API project serve users in Hong Kong?**
Meta's Geographic Use Policy lists Hong Kong SAR, as part of the People's Republic of China, as restricted for end users.
See [13-acceptable-use-and-data.md](13-acceptable-use-and-data.md).

**Where are the problem statements?**
No separate list is published (checked 2026-10-29); the two themes and two tracks are the frame. See
[05-problem-statements.md](05-problem-statements.md).
